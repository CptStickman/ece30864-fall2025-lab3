a. Pros:
1. Teams are able to be more flexible, responding better to changes in customer needs.
2. Teams are able to see where everyone is in development, are offer to assist if needed.
Cons:
1. Teams aren't able to plan too far ahead, working only a month or so at a time.
2. Teams need to be constantly communicating to make sure everyone is on the same page.

b. Scrum Master: The scrum master is in charge of maintaing the scrum setup, working to create 
sprints and assigning work to keep work from falling behind. They're the primary communicator 
in the team. 
Product Owner: The product owner works closely with the customers to ensure their needs are 
being addressed with the sprints. They likely add work to the backlog so the team knows they
need to work on it.
