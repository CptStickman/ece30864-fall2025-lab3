a. Pros:
1. Teams are able to be more flexible, responding better to changes in customer needs.
2. Teams are able to see where everyone is in development, are offer to assist if needed.
Cons:
1. Teams aren't able to plan too far ahead, working only a month or so at a time.
2. Teams need to be constantly communicating to make sure everyone is on the same page.

b. Scrum Master: The scrum master is in charge of maintaing the scrum setup, working to create 
sprints and assigning work to keep work from falling behind. They're the primary communicator 
in the team. 
Product Owner: The product owner works closely with the customers to ensure their needs are 
being addressed with the sprints. They likely add work to the backlog so the team knows they
need to work on it.

c. Sprint Planning Meeting:
1. Establish what needs to be accomplished during the sprint
2. Assign tasks to different team members.
Daily Standup Meeting:
1. Discuss what was accomplished yesterday
2. What needs to be accomplished today
Sprint Review Meeting
1. Discuss what was accomplished over the sprint
2. Based on feedback, add needed work to the backlog to be completed in a future sprint.

d. Epic: The big overall features. These often take multiple sprints to fully implement.
Story: Smaller features that combined equal one epic. They don't take as long, though they could comprimise an entire sprint.
Bug: Problems occuring with a task or story that need to be fixed. May be vital an needed to completed immediately, could be situational and done when not busy.
Task: Very narrow objectives that take a short ammount of time to complete, likely at most a few days. multiple of these comprise a story.
